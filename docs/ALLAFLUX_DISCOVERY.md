# ALLAFLUX™ Discovery & Audience Engine

ALLAFLUX should treat discovery as a first-class product capability rather than a side effect of uploads.

## Discovery Inputs

Rank and personalize catalog presentation using permitted signals such as:

- Recent plays
- Repeat listening
- Completion/retention
- Likes/favorites
- Follows
- Artist affinity
- Genre affinity
- Release freshness
- Playlist membership
- Search behavior
- Geographic or language context where lawfully collected and appropriately disclosed

## Discovery Surfaces

- Featured artists
- New releases
- Trending tracks
- Recommended tracks
- Genre browsing
- Artist discovery
- Album/release grids
- Playlist discovery
- Search
- Personalized library

## Artist-Centered Strategy

The platform should reinforce recognizable artist identities rather than treating every upload as an isolated object. Artist images, names, genres, languages, identity statements, and recurring creative cues should remain consistent across cards, artist pages, music pages, videos, and creator workflows.

## YouTube / External Media Alignment

ALLATYME's documented audience strategy emphasizes artist identity, emotionally specific storytelling, recognizable openings, Shorts discovery, and long-form catalog depth. ALLAFLUX should therefore support reusable metadata and media packages that can feed those publishing workflows without claiming direct control over third-party recommendation algorithms.

## Ranking Principles

1. Relevance before novelty.
2. Artist diversity to avoid over-concentration.
3. Freshness without destroying catalog discovery.
4. Transparent, measurable ranking signals.
5. No fabricated engagement.
6. No artificial play inflation.
7. Respect user controls and privacy requirements.

## Future Flux Services

Potential services:

- `discovery-api`
- `recommendation-worker`
- `search-index`
- `artist-affinity-service`
- `catalog-ranking-service`
- `analytics-events`

These should consume canonical catalog and analytics data rather than maintaining conflicting artist records.
